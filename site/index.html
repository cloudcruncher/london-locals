HOW IT WORKS

Three taps from your sofa to your local.

01

Prove you live here

Sign up with your postcode, then verify once (method to be confirmed before launch). Only verified residents will get the pass, which is why local businesses can trust the scheme.

02

Get your pass

At launch your pass will live in Apple or Google Wallet, with a rotating QR code designed to stop screenshots being reused. No paper cards.

03

Show it, save, repeat

Show it at the till. Perks are designed around quiet times and first visits, so venues welcome you and you save without the small print.
