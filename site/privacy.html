Privacy notice (draft)

Early-access notice. Not yet legally reviewed. Data controller: Robin Saini, sole trader, London. Contact: robinsaini156@gmail.com. ICO registration number: [to be added once registered].

What we collect

Name, email, postcode and borough (residents); business name, contact, postcode and type (businesses). Nothing else on this site collects personal data. The site sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers. If privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Plausible) is switched on, this notice will say so. Sign-up forms are processed by our form provider, named here before launch. Not used: third-party trackers. Our web host (GitHub Pages) processes visitor IP addresses in its server logs. If a form backend is not yet connected, a submission is held only in your own browser storage.

Why

To tell you when Locals launches in your borough, to decide launch order, and to contact businesses who applied. Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time. When the full service launches, businesses will see aggregated redemption counts and spend; any further disclosure of who redeemed will be covered by an updated notice before it happens.

Who sees it

Only the Locals team and our form and email providers acting on our instructions. We will never sell personal data. Sponsors and councils receive anonymised, aggregated figures only.

Keeping it

Until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, objection, and complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office.
