Read this before believing any number. Penetration, conversion, redemption frequency, new-customer share, sponsor and council income are untested assumptions. The first job of the pilot is to replace them with measured values. Costs assume a lean team (about £260k fixed plus £20k per borough) and exclude founder pay beyond that.

Locals (working name): verified-resident rewards for London high streets. Early access, launching in Hackney, Lewisham and Lambeth.

Map and venue counts: Food Standards Agency hygiene register (Open Government Licence v3.0), processed in the London Pulse project. Not affiliated with the FSA or any council.
